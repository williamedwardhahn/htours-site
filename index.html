What you'll actually do

Most of what visitors know about New Bern fits on a plaque. This walk is the version you'd get from a neighbor — the pharmacist who accidentally built a global brand and then went broke, the royal governor's palace that burned and came back, the fire in 1922 that redrew the town in a single day, and the rivers that explain why any of it is here.

You'll walk about a mile and a quarter on flat sidewalks, stop nine times, and finish at the water with somewhere good to eat and a reason to go there.
